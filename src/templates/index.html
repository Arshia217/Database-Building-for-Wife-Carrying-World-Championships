Do you know where wife carrying originated from?

The legend of Wife Carrying dates back to the late 1800s and the infamous robber Her Rosvo-Ronkainen. Ronkainen accepted only men into his band who proved their worth in an arduous track test carrying heavy sacks or live animals through the forests of Sonkajärvi. Today, couples from all over the world travel to Finland to test their grit, agility, and humor on the world-famous 253.5-meter obstacle course!
